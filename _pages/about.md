I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), advised by Professor [Noseong Park](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=VSuM3gYAAAAJ&hl=en) in the [Big Data Analytics Lab (BigDyL)](https://sites.google.com/view/npark/home).

My research focuses on **tabular data**, spanning **generation, representation learning, and foundation models**. Recently, I have been exploring **in-context learning** approaches for tabular data generation. I am also broadly interested in **data privacy** and **adversarial robustness**.

Before joining KAIST, I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Yonsei University (advised by Prof. Noseong Park), where my thesis *STaSy: Score-based Tabular Data Synthesis* was published as a Spotlight paper at ICLR 2023. I completed my B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics at Sookmyung Women's University.
