I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at the Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST), advised by Professor Noseong Park in the Big Data Analytics Lab (BigDyL).

My research focuses on tabular data, spanning generation, representation learning, and foundation models. Recently, I have been exploring in-context learning approaches for tabular data generation. I am also broadly interested in data privacy and adversarial robustness.

More specifically, my current interests include:

Research Interests

  • Tabular data generation
  • Representation learning and foundation models for tabular data
  • In-context learning for tabular data generation
  • Data privacy and adversarial robustness

Before joining KAIST, I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence from Yonsei University (advised by Prof. Noseong Park), where my thesis STaSy: Score-based Tabular Data Synthesis was published as a Spotlight paper at ICLR 2023. I completed my B.S. in Computer Science and Statistics at Sookmyung Women’s University.

You are always welcome to reach out — the best way to contact me is by email.

Selected publications

  1. 2024
    ICML
    Polynomial-based Self-Attention for Table Representation Learning
    Jayoung Kim, Yehjin Shin, Noseong Park
    Tabular Data Representation Learning Self-Attention
    ABS

    We propose a polynomial-based self-attention mechanism tailored to table representation learning, mitigating oversmoothing in deep Transformer-based tabular models.

  2. 2023
    ICML
    CoDi: Co-evolving Contrastive Diffusion Models for Mixed-type Tabular Synthesis
    Chaejeong Lee, Jayoung Kim, Noseong Park
    Tabular Data Diffusion Models Generative Models
    ABS

    CoDi co-evolves two diffusion models, one for continuous and one for discrete variables, coupled through contrastive learning to synthesize mixed-type tabular data.

  3. 2023
    ICLR
    STaSy: Score-based Tabular data Synthesis
    Jayoung Kim, Chaejeong Lee, Noseong Park
    Tabular Data Score-based Models Generative Models
    ABS

    STaSy applies score-based generative modeling to tabular data synthesis, introducing self-paced learning and fine-tuning strategies that stabilize training and improve sampling quality.

  4. 2021
    KDD
    SOS: Score-based Oversampling for Tabular Data
    Jayoung Kim, Chaejeong Lee, Yehjin Shin, Sewon Park, Minjung Kim, Noseong Park, Jihoon Cho
    Tabular Data Generative Models Imbalanced Learning
    ABS

    SOS uses score-based generative models to oversample minority classes in imbalanced tabular datasets.
